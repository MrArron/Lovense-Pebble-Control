## Short description

Control your Lovense toy discreetly from your wrist.

## Full description

Control your Lovense toy discreetly from your wrist. Choose an open Basic
remote-control face, or a Discrete face (Analog or Digital) that looks like
an ordinary watch to everyone but you — vibration level and pattern are
read entirely from hand position and color, never a number on screen.
Fully customizable colors, with a Toy Settings screen for connection status
and multi-toy groups.

This app is created independently by its developer and is not affiliated
with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Lovense or Pebble/Core Devices/Rebble.

Built with the assistance of Claude (Claude Sonnet 5, Anthropic).
